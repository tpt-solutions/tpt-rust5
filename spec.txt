DESIGN DOCUMENT 5: tpt-rust-5 (THE REFLEXES & SAFETY GUARDRAILS)
PROJECT OVERVIEW - https://github.com/tpt-solutions/tpt-protocol
tpt-rust-5 is the final safety and control layer. Before the Brain's optimal setpoints are sent to the physical hardware, the Reflexes layer ensures the commands are mechanically safe, logically permitted, and properly prioritized.
ECOSYSTEM GAP JUSTIFICATION
Rust has basic PID crates, but it has almost zero infrastructure for industrial safety guardrails, interlocks, priority arbitration, and safe actuation gating. This is the most desperate gap for physical control systems; without it, automated optimization can destroy physical hardware.
CORE PRINCIPLES
Safety Over Optimization: An E-stop or mechanical interlock will always override the Brain's most profitable mathematical solution.
Mechanical Empathy: Actuators have physical limits. Valves cause water hammer. Motors draw inrush current. Rate limiters and deadbands must protect the hardware.
Absolute Auditability: Every blocked, modified, or executed command must have a cryptographic or strictly logged reason code.
CRATE INVENTORY
tpt-control-action
Core traits for physical setpoints, discrete states (ON/OFF/FAULT), and continuous values (0-100%), mapped to TUP command envelopes.
tpt-control-limiter
Saturation, deadbands, and hysteresis logic for physical actuators to prevent rapid cycling and mechanical wear.
tpt-control-ratelimit
Slew-rate limiters and ramp-generators to prevent mechanical shock (e.g., preventing valve water hammer or motor inrush currents).
tpt-safety-interlock
Boolean logic engine for permissives and blocking conditions (e.g., "Do not start pump if suction pressure < X").
tpt-safety-envelope
Alarm generation, E-stop logic, and safe-state degradation paths when sensors fail or power is lost.
tpt-control-dryrun
Shadow execution engine that compares a new control policy against the current live state without actually sending the TUP command to the hardware.
tpt-actuation-gate
The final pre-flight check that validates a TUP command envelope against all safety limits and interlocks before handing it to the tpt-protocol daemon.
tpt-state-machine
Deterministic, auditable Finite State Machines (FSMs) for complex hardware sequences (e.g., boiler purge/start/stop sequences).
tpt-control-priority
Priority arbitration logic for competing control sources (Safety > Manual Override > Auto-Optimization > Schedule).
tpt-control-audit
Reason-code logging for exactly why a specific setpoint was chosen, modified, or blocked by the safety guardrails.
TUP INTEGRATION & DATA FLOW
tpt-rust-5 sits between the Brain and the Senses.
Read Path: Receives proposed TUP Command Envelopes from tpt-dispatch-solve. Reads live hardware state from tpt-state-snapshot.
Write Path: Passes validated, safe TUP Commands to tpt-protocol-daemon for physical execution.
